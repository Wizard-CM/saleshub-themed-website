From three folding chairs, an entire network.

“If you can move it cold from Esbjerg to Kathmandu without losing the head on the pour, the rest is just paperwork.”
— Rajan K., founder

In 2014, two brothers and a logistics nerd quit their day jobs to build the country's first cold-chain-first beverage distributor. The bet was unfashionable: spend on refrigerated trucks, climate-controlled bays, and a 24-hour stock turn before chasing volume.

Twelve years later, every bottle we touch has a serial number and a temperature log. Every truck has a GPS bead. Every label on our roster signs a freshness pledge before we shake on a contract — and we've walked away from more deals than we've signed.

The pledge is simple: if it doesn't taste the way the brewmaster meant it to, we don't carry it.
